Moin Mermaid Previewer

💡 Quick Guide

  • Ctrl/Cmd + Enter: Show Preview
  • Drag & Drop: Load a `.mmd` or `.txt` file
  • F: Toggle Fullscreen (while in preview)
  • Esc: Close Preview
  • Arrows / Scroll: Pan around diagram
  • Ctrl/Cmd + Scroll / Pinch: Zoom diagram
